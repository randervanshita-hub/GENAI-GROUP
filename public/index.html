    How it works

    Five steps between your question
    and a note you can check.

    1. 01

      Plan

      A fast model breaks the question into searches and spots buy/sell or off-topic requests.

      Gemini Flash-Lite
    2. 02

      Read

      An open-source model finds the most relevant passages across the annual report and earnings call.

      RAG · bge-small · pgvector
    3. 03

      Calculate

      Ratios like ROE, margins and P/E are worked out in code from the filings and a live NSE price.

      Tools · no AI arithmetic
    4. 04

      Write

      The model drafts the note from the evidence only. Every claim must cite a passage or a calculated figure.

      Gemini Flash-Lite
    5. 05

      Fact-check

      Each claim is judged against the exact source it cites. Anything unsupported is removed, in the open.

      Gemini Flash-Lite
