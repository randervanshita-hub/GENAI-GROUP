    How Arthavid works

    RAG

    Reads the filings

    Annual reports and earnings-call transcripts are split into passages, embedded, and stored in a vector database. Each question pulls back the most relevant pages.

    Tools

    Uses a calculator, not guesses

    Ratios like ROE, margins and P/E are computed in code from figures in the filings and a live price feed. The model never does the arithmetic.

    Workflow

    Five steps, three models

    A cheap model plans and checks; a stronger one writes. If a model is overloaded, the next in line takes over.

    Verification

    Checks itself

    Every claim is judged against the exact passage it cites. Unsupported claims are removed, and you can see which and why.

    Arthavid is an educational research tool, not investment advice. It does not make buy, sell or hold recommendations and is not a SEBI-registered investment adviser or research analyst. Figures come from company filings and may contain extraction errors. Always read the source.
